The Star Wars RPG Guide

The Star Wars Roleplaying Game from Fantasy Flight Games, now with Edge Studio, tells stories of smugglers, rebels and Force users with dice that answer more than "did it work?" Every roll also tells you what else happened along the way. This guide covers Edge of the Empire, Age of Rebellion and Force and Destiny, which share one rules system. It takes you from your first character through combat, starships and the Force to running a campaign, or playing alone.

The Star Wars RPG at a Glance

Four ideas carry most of the game. Each part of the guide builds on them.

Narrative Dice

Seven custom dice. Your skill and characteristic build the green and yellow dice; the GM sets the purple and red ones; blue and black dice cover circumstances.

Success and Advantage

Failures cancel Successes and Threats cancel Advantages. You can succeed with Threat or fail with Advantage, so every roll has a "but".

Triumph and Despair

The yellow and red dice can show a Triumph or a Despair: a Success or Failure that also brings a big turn of fortune.

Obligation, Duty, Morality

Each core book gives characters a story engine: the debts of the Outer Rim, service to the Rebellion, or the pull between light and dark.
